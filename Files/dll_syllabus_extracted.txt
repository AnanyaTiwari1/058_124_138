Introduction:
The  Deep  Learning  Laboratory  course  provides  a  hands-on  environment  to  implement  and
experiment with core concepts and advanced techniques in deep learning. This lab emphasizes
practical skills in designing, training, and optimizing neural networks using popular frameworks
such  as  TensorFlow  and PyTorch.  Students  will  work  on  real-world  problems,  exploring
applications like image recognition, natural language processing, and generative models. Through
guided experiments and projects, learners will gain proficiency in configuring architectures, tuning
hyperparameters, and deploying deep learning solutions, preparing them for research or industry
roles in AI and machine learning.
Course Objectives:
Gain practical experience in implementing and training deep learning models using popular
frameworks like TensorFlow.
Explore real-world applications of deep learning in fields such as computer vision, natural
language processing, and generative models.
Develop skills in optimizing deep learning architectures, including hyperparameter tuning
and model evaluation.
Build  and  deploy  end-to-end  deep  learning  projects,  from data  preprocessing  to  model
deployment, for real-world problem-solving.
Course Duration: The course duration is 40 sessions of 60 minutes each.
